Drop your astronomy images in this folder.

Supported: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
Any size — they'll be auto-cropped to 16:9.
Reference them by filename in ../../content.csv

Example: if you save a file here as "saturn.jpg", add a line to content.csv like:
    saturn.jpg,Saturn has the most spectacular ring system in our solar system.

You can delete this .txt file once you've added images.
