Drop your background music here as: background.mp3

The script looks for exactly this filename. If you have a different format
(e.g. .wav, .m4a), either convert it to mp3 or change MUSIC_PATH at the top
of make_video.py.

Pick something royalty-free for YouTube — try YouTube Audio Library,
Pixabay Music, or Free Music Archive.

You can delete this .txt file once you've added music.
